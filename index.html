ABOUT SMS LABORATORY

About Our Lab

SMS Laboratory at Hannam University conducts research on smart construction materials and structures. Our research focuses on the development of high-performance and functional construction materials, the evaluation of electrical responses under mechanical and environmental changes, and the application of sustainable materials for future infrastructure.
